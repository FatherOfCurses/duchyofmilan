# Design direction — duchyofmilan.llc (modern corporate, 2026)

Goal: sleek, powerful, faintly menacing. A 2026 AI/fintech company that has clearly won and
wants you to feel small. Everything the `.org` site was, inverted.

## Mood
Cold confidence. Expensive minimalism. "We already own the outcome."

## Signature modern cues
- Dark, high-contrast palette; lots of black and near-black with a single sharp accent.
- Huge, tight, tracked-out headlines; heavy weights.
- Thin hairline rules, glassmorphism, subtle gradients/glows.
- Animated metric counters and an up-and-to-the-right chart.
- Monospace for data/metrics/token figures.
- Generous whitespace; grid discipline; zero warmth.

## Palette
- Base: near-black (#0a0a0b) and graphite (#16171a)
- Surface: dark glass (#1c1e22 with blur)
- Accent: one cold, electric color — recommend electric violet (#7c5cff) or acid green
  (#8dff5a). Pick one and commit.
- Text: off-white (#f2f3f5) and muted gray (#9aa0a6)

## Type
- Headlines: a tight grotesk (Inter/Space Grotesk/Söhne feel), heavy weight.
- Data/metrics: a monospace (JetBrains Mono / IBM Plex Mono).

## Layout
- Full-bleed dark hero with a metric bar.
- Product/platform section as a cold "system" grid (ARIEL, DUCAT, ORACLE, CALIBAN).
- Leadership as austere portrait cards; the vacant founder's chair is a deliberate blank.
- Investor section with animated stats and a rising chart.

## Feel test
If it looks like a Series-D AI company that would make Prospero's `.org` weep, it's right.
The uncanny bonus: it should feel like the SAME logo/wordmark as `.org`, grown cold.
