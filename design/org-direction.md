# Design direction — duchyofmilan.org (Web 2.0, ~2006–2011)

Goal: earnest, handmade, optimistic. It should feel like a beloved early-web startup that
genuinely believed the internet would set people free.

## Mood
Sunny, communal, a little naive. "Startup in a garage." Trustworthy and human.

## Signature Web 2.0 cues (use tastefully, not as parody)
- Glossy gradients and soft drop shadows; big rounded corners.
- A "beta" badge near the logo (starburst or ribbon).
- Reflective/"aqua" buttons; subtle bevels.
- Friendly, rounded sans-serif headlines; generous sizes.
- Tag-cloud, guestbook, and RSS flourishes.
- Bright, saturated accent color(s).

## Palette
- Primary: sky/optimistic blue (#3b8bd6 / #2f6fb0)
- Accent: fresh green (#5bb85b) and warm orange (#f0952f)
- Background: near-white with a faint gradient (#f7fbff → #eaf3fb)
- Text: dark slate (#1f2d3d)

## Type
- Headlines: rounded geometric sans (e.g., Quicksand, Baloo, or "Helvetica Rounded" feel).
- Body: clean humanist sans (Verdana/Lucida-era warmth, or Nunito Sans).

## Layout
- Centered, fixed-ish width column (~960px), the classic Web 2.0 grid.
- Big hero with a friendly illustration or gradient.
- Rounded "cards" with soft shadows for the three pillars.
- Footer with a hand-drawn/"made with love" note.

## Feel test
If it looks like it could have won a "Web 2.0 startup of the year" badge in 2008, it's right.
