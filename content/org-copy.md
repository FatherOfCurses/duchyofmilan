## 2. Our Story

**Heading:** How the Duchy began

In a garage full of secondhand servers and too much coffee, our founder **Prospero** asked a
simple question: *what if the most powerful technology in the world belonged to the people who
use it?*

Duchy of Milan started as a wiki, a forum, and a promise — that no idea would be locked away,
no voice would be too small, and no barrier would stand between a person and the future they
wanted to build. What began as a handful of tinkerers became a city of them.

We named ourselves a "duchy" as a joke that became a mission: a place with its own rules,
governed by its people, where the old institutions held no power. Prospero still calls every
member a citizen. We think he means it.

**Pull quote (Prospero):** "Give ordinary people extraordinary tools, and watch them
astonish you."

## 4. Manifesto

**Heading:** The Duchy Manifesto

We believe technology, at its best, is a great equalizer.
We believe the walls between people are built by those who profit from them.
We believe knowledge shared is knowledge multiplied.
We believe the future should not be sold back to the people who build it.
We believe a small group of committed neighbors can still change everything.

We are not a company. We are not an audience. We are a duchy — and its citizens are its rulers.

**Signed:** Prospero & the citizens of Milan
