## 2. Company

**Heading:** About Duchy of Milan LLC

Duchy of Milan LLC is a diversified technology holding company operating at the intersection
of artificial intelligence, digital assets, and predictive markets. We acquire attention,
model behavior, and convert both into compounding returns for our stakeholders.
